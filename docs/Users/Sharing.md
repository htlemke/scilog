# Sharing the data

## Linking to an entry

The menu of every entry (⋮) has **Copy link**. The link opens the entry on a
page of its own, `https://<scilog>/entries/<id>`, with its comments below
it. Someone who is not logged in logs in first and then sees the entry
directly.

The link works for everyone who may read the entry. If they may also open
the logbook, the page leads there.
