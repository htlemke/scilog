# Sharing the data

## Linking to an entry

The menu of every entry (⋮) has **Copy link**. The link opens the entry on a
page of its own, `https://<scilog>/entries/<id>`, with its comments below
it. Someone who is not logged in logs in first and then sees the entry
directly.

The link works for everyone who may read the entry. If they may also open
the logbook, the page leads there.

## Sharing an entry with everyone logged in

Who may read an entry is normally decided by its logbook. To show one entry
to someone outside the logbook's groups, for example to support staff,
choose **Share with everyone logged in** in the entry's menu. Everyone who
can log in to SciLog may then read that entry, its comments and the files it
shows, but nothing else of the logbook. The link to the entry is copied at
the same time; a globe marks shared entries.

**Stop sharing with everyone** in the same menu undoes it. Both need the
right to edit the entry, but no editing time: older entries can be shared
too.
